Generated on 2026-10-05 with the built-in `image_gen` tool under the approved [2.5D office contract](../ZURI-2.5D-OFFICE.md). No CLI/API fallback or third-party character assets were used. ## Identity and pose contract

There are **three distinct generated adult anatomies, not fifteen independently generated people**: an Asian man, a Black woman and a gray-haired White man. Each base has five garment-color variants, mapped to all fifteen stable avatar IDs in `characters-profiles.json`. Display names and saved IDs remain unchanged. The original blue garments are selectively recolored at runtime; skin, hair, trousers, shoes and source alpha are retained. Reflections share anatomy and are explicitly part of the contract.

The base atlas contains three rows × ten poses: front southeast idle/contact stride/initial second stride/seated A/seated B, then back northwest versions. The initially generated two walking poses were too similar to confidently use as opposing gait phases. The supplementary atlas supplies three rows × two substantially different bent-knee/arm-swing walking poses, front southeast and back northwest. The unused original second-stride columns remain in the unchanged original file.

Runtime frames use 128 × 224 canvases, centered feet at the bottom. Standing body height targets 208 native pixels; seated art retains its shorter body height and contains no baked chair. The scene owns world display size. Four logical direction rows are down=southwest (reflected front), up=northeast (reflected back), right=southeast (front), left=northwest (back), derived from the actual 2:1 projection and recorded in `characters-directions.json`. Each row has seven slots: idle, contact stride, bent-knee stride, seated A/B, read A/B. Read reuses seated hand poses; it does **not** claim a separately generated reading animation. UI portraits crop the bust from this same generated person and garment palette.

## Inspection and limits

- Visual inspection: three consistent adult identities; natural proportions, detailed fabric/hair and distinct seated geometry; no pixel, voxel or chibi treatment. The supplementary stride changes limb geometry, not merely sprite position or bobbing. These are compact generated pose cycles, not motion-captured or skeletally interpolated animation; live gait smoothness remains an integration check.
- Alpha inspection used read-only `System.Drawing.Bitmap.GetPixel`. Base: 1,013,161 fully transparent pixels, 86,818 alpha 1–15 pixels, 473,559 pixels with alpha ≥16. Stride: 1,245,372 fully transparent pixels, 35,390 alpha 1–15 pixels, 292,102 pixels with alpha ≥16. Counts sum to the complete dimensions.
- The tool preview displays residual RGB data in fully transparent pixels (including the alternate atlas's apparent backdrop). Sampled background positions have alpha 0. Apparently bright red base-image fringes have maximum alpha 2; they are not opaque outlines. Alpha is preserved by runtime canvas composition. Final appearance over actual office surfaces must be assessed in the desktop screenshots, rather than treating the tool's raw preview as an opaque backdrop.
- Unit checks lock the exact original hashes, RGBA format, all fifteen ID mappings, five unique variants per base, crop bounds and atlas dimensions. They do not establish live GPU rendering, selection, seated alignment or performance.
- At most twenty distinct normalized canvas textures are retained per avatar (read aliases seated frames). Fifteen loaded avatars therefore require approximately 32.8 MiB of canvas pixel storage plus a similar GPU texture allocation, before source-image and engine overhead. This is a calculated bound, not a measured process-memory claim.

## Prompt set
